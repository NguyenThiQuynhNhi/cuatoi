5. Governance Notes
Privacy, scope and limitations
  • Admin does NOT access private agent–client message content through monetisation screens.
  • All analytics and monetisation views for messages are strictly aggregated or anonymised where required.
  • Admin manages configurations only: which features exist, who can see them, and high-level thresholds.
  • Actual enforcement of business rules will depend on contracts and compliance review with the client.
Any additional governance requirement (e.g. data retention, jurisdiction-specific rules) must be confirmed with client and legal teams.
